# Article Header Media Discoverability Decision

Status: recorded and triaged

Date: 2026-09-08

The generated SmartDox article page currently makes its registered video,
summary-slides PDF, and article PDF difficult to discover, and it does not
provide an article-page infographic link or inline infographic projection.
The requested direction is to place available media actions as buttons below
the title, while reserving the same article-header area for compact symbolic
metadata such as tags. A registered infographic should also appear directly
below the effective LEAD.

The direction applies to SmartDox's shared DoxSite projection rather than to a
single site theme. Cozy-built BoKs must receive the same behavior through the
existing publication-metadata boundary, without a Cozy-only HTML workaround.

The exploratory layout and ownership proposal is recorded in
`docs/notes/article-header-metadata-and-media-actions.md`.

## Development Candidate

### DEV-005: Article header metadata and media actions

Promote and implement a stable article-header region with a compact metadata
strip and button-styled actions for available video, summary-slides PDF,
article PDF, and infographic roles. Project the infographic as an inline
figure immediately below the effective LEAD, preserve existing registry and
Notice behavior, and prove that Cozy BoK generation consumes the same common
SmartDox projection.
