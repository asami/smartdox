## Accepted SmartDox Contract

The authority is `docs/design/article-media-publication.md` and
`docs/spec/article-media-publication.md`.

- `article_pdf` and `summary_slides_pdf` are explicit localized document roles
  with a site-visible `public_path`, `application/pdf` media type, optional
  label, deterministic duplicate/absence behavior, and exact-locale resolution
  without filename inference.
- SmartDox parses the roles into normalized article-media variants and projects
  only the resolved locale's available references into ordinary article media
  and both global and category-local Notice media maps.
- A nonblank label is retained verbatim; an absent label uses the existing
  locale default. Blank-only labels are absent.
- Existing infographic, external-video, site-hosted-video, legacy
  `VideoPublication`, and no-media behavior remain compatible.
